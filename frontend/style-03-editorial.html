The Story of the Race

The gearbox that rewrote Hungary

A lap-57 retirement turned Oscar Piastri's afternoon — and McLaren's shot at a one-two finish — into Max Verstappen's masterclass in patience under yellow.

No exit kerbs. That's maybe what happened to Oscar's car.

Lando Norris radio to pit wall · lap 57

Piastri had been holding station in the fight for the podium when his McLaren slowed on the exit of Turn 4, a gearbox failure ending his race on the spot. Race control threw the Virtual Safety Car for two laps while marshals cleared the stricken car — two laps that reshuffled the entire complexion of the grand prix.

Strategy Insight Verstappen did not pit during the VSC to gain track position

It worked. Verstappen emerged from the sequence with clean air ahead of him and never looked back, crossing the line 15.3 seconds adrift of Norris but comfortably clear of the pack behind him — a race salvaged from what had looked, twenty laps out, like a home defeat.
