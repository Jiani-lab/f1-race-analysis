Incident — Lap 56 / 57

Virtual Safety Car deployed

Piastri retires from P2/P3 with gearbox failure

“No exit kerbs. That’s maybe what happened to Oscar’s car.” L. Norris — Team Radio, Lap 57

Piastri came to a stop on track on lap 57 while running second, promoting a strong points finish into a retirement and triggering a Virtual Safety Car for laps 56–57. McLaren has not confirmed the exact failure point; Norris’s own radio speculated the exit kerbs at Turn 4 as a contributing factor.

Verstappen elected to stay out during the VSC rather than pit, trading a tyre-life risk for track position — the call that ultimately delivered his P2 finish.

Penalty — Lap 68

“And we got a 5 second penalty for speeding into the pitlane” — Hamilton’s radio call. The penalty applied post-race, dropping him from P4 to P5 and promoting Leclerc to P4.
