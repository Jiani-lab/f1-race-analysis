What this actually is
Screen + audio, turned into race understanding
There's no official F1 timing data behind this — it just watches the broadcast the same way you do, reading the screen and listening to the commentary, and turns that into something more useful than staring at a lap-time chart.
Detect

Just start watching. Bilibili, YouTube, whatever you already watch races on — it recognizes a live F1 session from the on-screen lap counter on its own, nothing to switch on per broadcast.

Understand

Pit stops, safety cars, overtakes, team radio get pulled out and phrased into short, readable events as the race runs — not a wall of raw commentary text.

Notify

One real push notification when something involving your driver actually matters — not a ping for every lap. Works with zero tabs open once you've granted permission once.

How it works
Four things happen while you watch
01

Detect

Checks the screen every 90s for race-lap text. Recognizes the race on its own, no manual start.

02

Capture

Pulls what's on screen and what's being said, merges it into one timeline as the race runs.

03

Notify

Pings your phone or browser for the moments tied to your favorite driver — rare, on purpose.

04

Ask

Open Question anytime, mid-race or after, and ask what actually happened — grounded in this race's own data.

A couple of things worth knowing: this is still a personal project, not a public app — right now everyone who uses it sets up their own copy, there's no single shared site everyone logs into together.

It follows one race at a time, and it can't read tire-compound colors straight off the graphics yet — for now it works that out from commentary and on-screen subtitles instead.
